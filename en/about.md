# About

Hello! My name is Jose Luis Garcia, I am 28 years old and an Electronics Engineer graduated from Universidad Simón Bolívar (USB) in Caracas, Venezuela. I am currently pursuing a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam (UvA) and have more than four years of professional experience in developing digital solutions and implementing machine learning models.

I have worked on projects that combine applied AI, web development, and data analysis, collaborating with organizations such as the WHO, UNESCO, and the ILO, as well as with media outlets like El Tiempo Latino, where I led the adoption of artificial intelligence in their workflows. I have also designed deep learning models for healthcare, including a 3D CNN for the detection of respiratory pathologies in chest CT scans.

My main skills include:

- Languages: Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, SQL, MATLAB, and C.

- Frameworks & Libraries: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Django, Flask, Angular.

- Cloud & Tools: Microsoft Azure, AWS, Odoo ERP, WordPress, Git, Google Analytics.

In addition, I am the author and co-author of academic publications in the field of AI and computer vision, and I have complemented my training with certifications in Deep Learning, NLP, and Machine Learning.

I am passionate about the potential of artificial intelligence to improve people’s lives, especially in the fields of healthcare and information accessibility. I enjoy tackling challenging projects, learning quickly, and working both independently and as part of a team.

I am currently highly motivated to relocate to Madrid, with the goal of advancing my career, contributing to impactful projects, and becoming part of its vibrant tech community. I am a native Spanish speaker and fluent in English.
